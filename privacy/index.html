Aura Bot - Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Aura Bot handles information when you use the bot.

1. Information We Collect

Aura Bot may process Discord information that is required for the bot's features, such as Discord user IDs, server IDs, and command-related information.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used only to provide, maintain, and improve the features of Aura Bot.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may only be processed when necessary to provide the bot's features or comply with legal requirements.

4. Data Retention

Data is retained only for as long as necessary for the bot's features and operation.

5. Data Deletion

If you want information associated with your use of Aura Bot to be deleted, contact the Aura Bot administrators through the official Discord server.

6. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the Aura Bot administrators through the official Discord server.
